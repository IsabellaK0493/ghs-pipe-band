The band

Talent, teamwork
& tradition

The GHS Pipe Band is where Heriot's pupils learn the pipes, snare, tenor and bass drum, from their first practice chanter to the competition field.

Players from P6 to S6 march side by side. Our Juvenile band competes at the major championships, and finished 4th in the world this August, a place behind St Andrew's College from New Zealand. Our Novice Juvenile band is where newer players get their first taste of the circle, and in 2026 it came back into the prize lists at Dumbarton and Perth.

Away from competition, the band leads the Edinburgh Riding of the Marches up the Royal Mile, plays the CCF Beating Retreat at the Palace of Holyroodhouse, and performs at Remembrance, Christmas and school events through the year.

The 2026 season

  1. Mar Bessie Watson

    International Women's Day band

    The school's female pipers and drummers formed a band of their own and played at the plaque to Edinburgh piper Bessie Watson, just outside the school.
  2. May

    Beating Retreat at Holyroodhouse

    Rory W (S5) took the mace as Drum Major for the massed bands, leading the parade through the Palace grounds.
  3. Jul

    Scottish Championships, Dumbarton

    Novice Juveniles 6th, Juveniles 5th, playing their brand-new medley in competition for the first time.
  4. Aug

    Perth Highland Games

    A 3rd prize for the Novice Juvenile band, a week out from the Worlds.
  5. Aug

    World Pipe Band Championships

    The Juveniles placed 4th with their March, Strathspey & Reel. The Novice Juveniles marched into Arena 1, a stage normally kept for Grade 1.
  6. Sep

    Edinburgh Riding of the Marches

    Leading the procession up the Royal Mile again. Rory C (S6) played the lament and Ran (S6) led the VIP procession.
